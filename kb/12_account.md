# Managing your account

## Closing my account
<!-- intents: terminate_account -->
Before closing, move your money out and cancel direct debits and subscriptions linked to your cards. Then go to **Profile → Close account** and confirm. **Closing an account cannot be undone**: your cards stop working immediately and the account cannot be reopened. We send a final statement by email.

## Changing my personal details
<!-- intents: edit_personal_details -->
Update your email, phone number and address in **Profile → Personal details**. Changes to your name or date of birth need a new identity document, which you upload in the same place.

## Is there an age limit?
<!-- intents: age_limit -->
You must be at least 18 to open a standard account. Customers aged 13–17 can have a teen account opened and managed by a parent or guardian.

## Which countries do you support?
<!-- intents: country_support -->
You can open an account if you live in one of the countries listed in *supported countries*. If you move, update your address in the app: some features and card delivery options differ by country.

## My cash or cheque deposit is not showing
<!-- intents: balance_not_updated_after_cheque_or_cash_deposit -->
Cash added at a partner retail point appears within 1 business day; keep the receipt until it does. We do not accept cheques: cheques sent to us are returned to the sender. If a cash deposit has not appeared after 1 business day, send us a photo of the receipt from **Help → Missing deposit**.

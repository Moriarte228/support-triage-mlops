# Sending and receiving bank transfers

## How long does a transfer take?
<!-- intents: transfer_timing -->
Transfers to other Acme accounts are instant. Transfers in euro to European banks usually arrive within 1 business day. International transfers in other currencies take 1–5 business days. The first transfer to a new recipient may be held for a security check for up to 24 hours.

## My transfer is pending
<!-- intents: pending_transfer -->
A transfer is pending until it has been sent to the recipient's bank. Transfers made on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day. You can see the expected arrival date in the transfer details.

## My transfer failed or was declined
<!-- intents: failed_transfer, declined_transfer -->
A **declined** transfer was never sent: the usual reasons are not enough money, a limit, a security check or a recipient we cannot send to. No money leaves your account.

A **failed** transfer was sent but could not be completed, for example because the account number was wrong or the recipient's bank rejected it. The money returns to your account within 2–5 business days.

## Why can't I send money to this recipient?
<!-- intents: beneficiary_not_allowed -->
We cannot send money to some countries and to some types of businesses (see *supported countries*). Check that the recipient's account number and bank code are correct. If the recipient is allowed but the transfer is still blocked, contact support.

## Cancelling a transfer
<!-- intents: cancel_transfer -->
You can cancel a transfer only while it is still pending and has not been sent: open it and tap **Cancel transfer**, then confirm. Cancelling cannot be undone. Instant transfers between Acme accounts cannot be cancelled; ask the recipient to send the money back.

## The recipient hasn't received my transfer
<!-- intents: transfer_not_received_by_recipient -->
Check the transfer status and the recipient's details. International transfers can take up to 5 business days and may pass through intermediary banks. If the expected arrival date has passed, download the proof of payment from the transfer details and share it with the recipient's bank, or ask us to trace the transfer.

## Transfer fees
<!-- intents: transfer_fee_charged -->
Transfers in the same currency to European banks are free. International transfers and transfers that need a currency exchange may carry a fee (see *fee schedule*). Intermediary or recipient banks can also deduct their own fees, which we do not control.

## Receiving money into my account
<!-- intents: receiving_money, transfer_into_account -->
Share your account details from **Accounts → Account details**: account holder name, account number and bank code. Incoming euro transfers from European banks usually arrive the same business day; international transfers can take longer and may have intermediary fees deducted.

## My balance didn't update after a bank transfer
<!-- intents: balance_not_updated_after_bank_transfer -->
Incoming transfers can take up to 1 business day to appear. Check that the sender used your exact account details. If the money has not arrived after 3 business days, ask the sender for proof of payment and send it to us from **Help → Missing transfer**.

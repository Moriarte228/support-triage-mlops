# Refunds from merchants

## How do I get a refund?
<!-- intents: request_refund -->
Ask the merchant for a refund first: we cannot issue a refund on a merchant's behalf. If the goods never arrived, were faulty, or the merchant refuses a refund it promised, open a dispute from the transaction screen within 120 days of the payment. Attach any messages or receipts you have.

## My refund isn't showing up
<!-- intents: refund_not_showing_up -->
Refunds from merchants usually take 5–10 business days to appear and show as a separate transaction, not as a change to the original payment. If the merchant confirmed the refund and it has not appeared after 15 business days, send us the merchant's confirmation from the transaction screen.

# Identity verification and source of funds

## Why do I need to verify my identity?
<!-- intents: why_verify_identity -->
Banks are required by law to know who their customers are, to prevent fraud and money laundering. Verification also protects your account if someone else tries to open or use it. Until you are verified, some features and limits are restricted.

## How do I verify my identity?
<!-- intents: verify_my_identity -->
Go to **Profile → Verify identity**. Take a photo of a valid passport, national ID card or residence permit, then record a short selfie video. Most checks finish within minutes; some take up to 24 hours.

## I can't verify my identity
<!-- intents: unable_to_verify_identity -->
The most common reasons are a blurry photo, glare on the document, an expired document, or a name that does not match your profile. Retake the photos in good light without flash, with all four corners of the document visible. After 3 failed attempts, your verification is passed to our team for a manual review.

## Why are you asking where my money comes from?
<!-- intents: verify_source_of_funds -->
For large or unusual incoming payments we may ask you to show where the money came from, for example a payslip, a sale contract or a bank statement. This is a legal requirement. Some account features may be limited until we review the documents, which usually takes 2 business days.

# Lost, stolen or compromised cards, PIN and passcode

## My card is lost or stolen
<!-- intents: lost_or_stolen_card -->
**Freeze the card immediately** in **Cards → Freeze**. Freezing is instant and reversible: unfreeze it if you find the card.

If you are sure the card is gone, choose **Report lost or stolen**. This blocks the card permanently and cannot be undone; we order a replacement automatically. Check your recent transactions and dispute any payment you did not make.

## Someone used my card or my card details were stolen
<!-- intents: compromised_card -->
Freeze the card, then choose **Report as compromised**. We block the card permanently and send you a new one with a new number. Dispute each transaction you did not make from the transaction screen. Acme will never ask for your full card number, PIN or app passcode by phone, email or message.

## I lost my phone
<!-- intents: lost_or_stolen_phone -->
Your app is protected by your passcode or biometrics. Log in on a new device and the old device is signed out automatically. If you cannot log in, contact support from the website to freeze your cards and sign out all devices.

## My PIN is blocked
<!-- intents: pin_blocked -->
The PIN is blocked after 3 wrong attempts. Unblock it in **Cards → Security → Unblock PIN**, then make a chip-and-PIN payment or a balance check at an ATM with the correct PIN. You can view your PIN in the app at any time.

## Changing my PIN
<!-- intents: change_pin -->
You can change your PIN at ATMs that support PIN change in your country. Your PIN is always visible in **Cards → Security → Show PIN**.

## I forgot my app passcode
<!-- intents: passcode_forgotten -->
Tap **Forgot passcode** on the login screen. We verify your phone number and email, and may ask for a short selfie video, then you can set a new passcode.

# Currency exchange

## What exchange rate do you use?
<!-- intents: exchange_rate -->
We use a rate based on the interbank market rate, updated continuously on weekdays. A markup applies on weekends, when markets are closed (see *fee schedule*). You can see the current rate in the app before every exchange (see *current exchange rates*).

## Exchange fees
<!-- intents: exchange_charge -->
Exchanges are free up to your plan's monthly allowance. Above it, a percentage fee applies (see *fee schedule*). The fee, if any, is shown before you confirm.

## Exchanging money in the app
<!-- intents: exchange_via_app -->
Go to **Accounts → Exchange**, choose the currencies and the amount, and confirm. The exchange is instant and the money appears in your account in the new currency.

## Which currencies do you support?
<!-- intents: fiat_currency_support -->
You can hold, exchange and spend the currencies listed in *supported currencies*. We support only government-issued (fiat) currencies, not cryptocurrencies.

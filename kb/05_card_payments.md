# Card payments, fees and unrecognised transactions

## Why is my card payment pending?
<!-- intents: pending_card_payment -->
A payment stays pending until the merchant completes it, usually within 7 days. The money is reserved but not yet taken. If the merchant never completes the payment, the reserved amount is released back to your account automatically.

## My card payment was reverted
<!-- intents: reverted_card_payment -->
A reverted payment means the merchant cancelled it, so the money has been returned to your account. This often happens when a shop cancels an order or retries a payment. If you still want the item, contact the merchant.

## I was charged twice
<!-- intents: transaction_charged_twice -->
Often one of the two is a pending pre-authorisation that disappears once the final payment completes. Wait until both are no longer pending. If both are completed, ask the merchant for a refund first; if they refuse, dispute the duplicate from the transaction screen.

## I don't recognise a card payment
<!-- intents: card_payment_not_recognised -->
Merchant names on statements can differ from shop names; tap the transaction to see more details. If the payment is not yours, freeze your card, report it as compromised and dispute the payment (see *Someone used my card*).

## Why was I charged a fee on a card payment?
<!-- intents: card_payment_fee_charged -->
Card payments in your account currency are free. Payments in another currency are free up to your plan's monthly exchange allowance; above it, an exchange fee applies (see *fee schedule*). The fee is shown in the transaction details.

## The exchange rate on my card payment is wrong
<!-- intents: card_payment_wrong_exchange_rate -->
Card payments in another currency use the card network's rate at the moment the merchant completes the payment, not when you paid. That is why the final amount can differ slightly from the pending amount or from rates you see online. A markup applies on weekends (see *fee schedule*).

## There is an extra charge on my statement
<!-- intents: extra_charge_on_statement -->
Extra lines usually come from an ATM operator's fee, an exchange fee or a merchant surcharge. Tap the transaction to see what it is. If you still cannot explain it, contact support from the transaction screen.

## I don't recognise a direct debit
<!-- intents: direct_debit_payment_not_recognised -->
A direct debit is a payment a company collects from your account under an authorisation (mandate) you gave, for example for a subscription or utility bill. See all mandates in **Payments → Direct debits** and cancel any you don't want. If you never authorised the direct debit, request a refund from the transaction screen.

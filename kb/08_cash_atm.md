# Cash withdrawals and ATMs

## Where can I withdraw cash?
<!-- intents: atm_support -->
You can withdraw cash at any ATM showing the Visa logo. Some ATMs are free of the operator's own fee; see the *ATM directory* for locations by city.

## Cash withdrawal fees
<!-- intents: cash_withdrawal_charge -->
Withdrawals are free up to your plan's monthly allowance; above it, a fee applies (see *fee schedule*). The ATM operator may add its own fee, which is shown on the ATM screen before you confirm. We do not control or refund operator fees.

## My cash withdrawal was declined
<!-- intents: declined_cash_withdrawal -->
A withdrawal is declined when there is not enough money, the daily withdrawal limit is reached (see *limits*), the PIN is wrong, the card is frozen, or the ATM does not support the card. The app shows the reason in a notification.

## My cash withdrawal is pending
<!-- intents: pending_cash_withdrawal -->
A withdrawal stays pending until the ATM operator confirms it, usually within 1–3 days. If the ATM did not give you cash, the reserved amount is released back to your account automatically within 7 days.

## I received the wrong amount of cash
<!-- intents: wrong_amount_of_cash_received -->
Keep the ATM receipt if you have one and report the problem from the withdrawal in the app within 60 days. We ask the ATM operator to check the machine's records; this can take up to 45 days, and you are refunded if a shortfall is confirmed.

## I don't recognise a cash withdrawal
<!-- intents: cash_withdrawal_not_recognised -->
If you did not make the withdrawal, freeze your card, report it as compromised and dispute the withdrawal (see *Someone used my card*).

## The exchange rate on my withdrawal abroad is wrong
<!-- intents: wrong_exchange_rate_for_cash_withdrawal -->
Some ATMs offer to convert the amount into your home currency at their own rate. Decline this offer and choose to be charged in the local currency: then our rate applies (see *current exchange rates*). The rate is fixed when the operator completes the withdrawal, which can differ slightly from the moment you took the cash.

# Adding money (top-ups)

## Ways to add money
<!-- intents: top_up_by_cash_or_cheque -->
You can add money by bank transfer to your Acme account number, with a debit or credit card, or with Apple Pay or Google Pay. In some countries you can also add cash at partner retail points (see *supported countries*). We do not accept cheques.

## Fees for adding money
<!-- intents: top_up_by_bank_transfer_charge, top_up_by_card_charge -->
Adding money by bank transfer is free on our side, but the sending bank may charge its own fee. Adding money by card can carry a fee depending on the card type and the country where it was issued (see *fee schedule*). The fee is shown before you confirm.

## Adding money with a card
<!-- intents: topping_up_by_card -->
Go to **Accounts → Add money → Card**, enter the amount and confirm. The card must be in your name. Your card's bank may ask you to confirm the payment with 3-D Secure.

## My top-up is pending
<!-- intents: pending_top_up -->
Card top-ups are usually instant. Sometimes your card's bank or our security checks hold a top-up for up to 24 hours. Bank transfer top-ups arrive within 1 business day.

## My top-up failed
<!-- intents: top_up_failed -->
A top-up fails when your card's bank declines it, the 3-D Secure check is not completed, a limit is reached, or the card is not in your name. Usually no money is taken. If money was taken from your card, it is returned within 5 business days.

## My top-up was reverted
<!-- intents: top_up_reverted -->
A reverted top-up was cancelled after it was made, usually because the card's bank or our security checks rejected it. The money goes back to the card you used, within 5 business days.

## Verifying a top-up
<!-- intents: verify_top_up -->
When you add a new card, we may ask you to verify it. We make a small temporary charge with a 6-digit code in the description. Find the code in your card's banking app and enter it in **Accounts → Verify card**. The temporary charge is released automatically.

## Top-up limits
<!-- intents: top_up_limits -->
How much you can add per top-up, per day and per month depends on your plan and verification status (see *limits*).

## Automatic top-up
<!-- intents: automatic_top_up -->
Turn on **Auto top-up** in **Accounts → Add money**. Choose a threshold and an amount: when your balance falls below the threshold, we add the amount from your saved card. You can turn it off at any time.

# Card not working, declined payments and where cards are accepted

## My card is not working
<!-- intents: card_not_working -->
Check in the app that the card is activated, not frozen and not expired, and that you have not reached a spending limit. If the chip or magnetic stripe is damaged, order a replacement in **Cards → Replace card**. Your old card stops working as soon as the replacement is ordered.

## Contactless is not working
<!-- intents: contactless_not_working -->
Contactless starts working after your first chip-and-PIN payment. Each contactless payment has a maximum amount (see *limits*). For security, a terminal may ask for your PIN after several contactless payments in a row; enter it once and contactless will work again.

## Why was my card payment declined?
<!-- intents: declined_card_payment -->
The app shows the reason in a notification for every declined payment. The usual reasons are: not enough money in the account, a frozen card, a spending limit, a blocked merchant category in your card settings (for example gambling), or a security check. If none of these apply, contact support from the declined transaction.

## Where can I use my card?
<!-- intents: card_acceptance -->
You can pay wherever Visa is accepted, in shops and online, in most countries. Some merchants, such as fuel pumps, hotels and car rental companies, place a temporary hold (pre-authorisation) and may refuse virtual cards.

## Visa or Mastercard?
<!-- intents: visa_or_mastercard -->
Acme physical cards are Visa debit cards. Virtual and disposable cards are Mastercard debit cards.

## Apple Pay and Google Pay
<!-- intents: apple_pay_or_google_pay -->
You can add physical and virtual Acme cards to Apple Pay or Google Pay in **Cards → Add to wallet**. Disposable cards cannot be added because their number changes after every payment.

## Which cards and currencies can I use to add money?
<!-- intents: supported_cards_and_currencies -->
You can top up with Visa or Mastercard debit and credit cards issued in your name. The currencies you can hold and top up in are listed in *supported currencies*.

## An ATM kept my card
<!-- intents: card_swallowed -->
Freeze the card in the app straight away. Cards kept by ATMs are destroyed by the ATM operator for security, and we cannot get them back. Order a replacement in **Cards → Replace card**; your balance is not affected.

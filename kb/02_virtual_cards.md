# Virtual and disposable cards

## Getting a virtual card
<!-- intents: getting_virtual_card -->
A virtual card is created instantly in **Cards → New virtual card**. It has its own number, expiry date and security code, and works for online payments and with Apple Pay or Google Pay. Virtual cards cannot be used at ATMs.

## Disposable virtual cards
<!-- intents: get_disposable_virtual_card -->
A disposable virtual card gets a new number automatically after every payment, so its details are useless if a shop is hacked. Create one in **Cards → New disposable card**. Use it for one-off online purchases. It is not suitable for subscriptions, recurring payments or deposits (hotels, car rental), because the number changes.

## Limits for disposable cards
<!-- intents: disposable_card_limits -->
How many disposable cards you can create per day and the maximum amount per payment depend on your plan (see *limits*).

## My virtual card is not working
<!-- intents: virtual_card_not_working -->
Check that the card is not frozen and that you entered the billing address from your Acme profile. A disposable card number works only once: after a payment, use the new number shown in the app. Some merchants do not accept virtual cards for pre-authorisations or deposits; use a physical card there. If the card still fails, delete it and create a new one.

# Ordering, receiving and activating your card

## Ordering a physical card
<!-- intents: order_physical_card, get_physical_card -->
You can order a physical card in the app: **Cards → Order physical card**. Check that your delivery address is correct before you confirm. Whether delivery is free depends on your plan and country (see the *fee schedule*). Physical cards work in shops, online and at ATMs.

## Ordering an additional card
<!-- intents: getting_spare_card -->
You can hold more than one physical card at the same time, for example a spare card for travelling. Order it the same way: **Cards → Order physical card**. The maximum number of active cards depends on your plan (see *limits*).

## When will my card arrive?
<!-- intents: card_arrival, card_delivery_estimate -->
We dispatch cards within 1 business day of your order. Delivery time depends on the destination country (see *supported countries, delivery times*). You can track the status in **Cards → Card on its way**.

If your card has not arrived 5 business days after the estimated date, tap **My card hasn't arrived**. We will cancel the card in transit and send a new one free of charge. The cancelled card cannot be used even if it arrives later.

## Activating your card
<!-- intents: activate_my_card -->
Activate a new physical card in **Cards → Activate card** by entering the last 4 digits printed on it. Until it is activated, the card cannot be used for payments. Your first payment must be a chip-and-PIN payment; contactless payments work after that.

## My card is about to expire
<!-- intents: card_about_to_expire -->
We send a replacement card automatically about 30 days before the expiry date, as long as your account is active and your address is up to date. Your current card keeps working until the last day of the month shown on it. Card details change on the new card, so update them in subscriptions and online shops.

## Linking a card to the app
<!-- intents: card_linking -->
Acme cards appear in the app automatically once activated. If you found a card that you only **froze**, unfreeze it in **Cards → Unfreeze**. A card that you **reported as lost or stolen** is permanently blocked and cannot be linked again: order a replacement instead.
